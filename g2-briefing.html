The Solution

Three simple changes

1

Put two people on the soldering step instead of one. It's the biggest chunk of work — split it between two people and the waiting disappears.

2

Have one person prep materials ahead of time. Wires prepped in advance means the solderers never sit idle waiting for parts.

3

Run smaller batches. Instead of finishing 100 of a part before moving on, finish 50. Units get done sooner — the whole line speeds up.

None of this costs money. It's just who does what, and when.
